## What problem needs to be solved?
The hit position of "darts" (Velcro balls) on a dartboard shall be detected automatically to calculate scores and display game progress.

Project ideas:

* Detection of dart hit position
* Score calculation based on dartboard logic
* Different Game Modes
* Round and leg calculation
* Multiplayer
